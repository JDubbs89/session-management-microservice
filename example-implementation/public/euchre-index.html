How to play

Four seats, with bots filling vacancies when 1–3 humans play. Seats 1 and 3 face seats 2 and 4. Each player gets five cards from a 24-card deck. Bid on the turned card’s suit, or pass. In the second round, choose another suit; our house rule requires the dealer to choose if everyone passes.

The dealer picks up the turned card when ordered up and discards one. The jack of trump is highest, followed by the same-color jack (which counts as trump), then A, K, Q, 10, 9. Follow the led suit if you can. Three or four tricks earn the callers one point; all five earn two, or four when going alone. Defenders earn two by stopping the callers. First team to ten wins.

A departing guest is played by a bot for the rest of the game. The host leaving closes the table. Refreshing leaves your seat; sign-in is restored, but an active hand cannot be rejoined. Euchre rules reference.
